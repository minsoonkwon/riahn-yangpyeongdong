From dandy cuts
to layered cuts

At RIAHN Hair Yangpyeong-dong, a women's cut is ₩20,000 and a men's cut is ₩18,000. A design cut (shampoo included) is ₩30,000, a men's design cut ₩25,000, and a detox cut ₩40,000. A junior cut (up to middle school) is ₩16,000 and a kids' cut (under 5) is ₩25,000.

Our designers specialize in dandy cuts and layered cuts. Your designer consults with you first, then styles to suit the look you want. For volume or waves, see our perms & straightening page too.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a neat men's dandy cut
  • Anyone who wants a light layered cut with movement
  • Parents booking cuts for children (junior and kids' cuts)
  • Anyone who wants a blow-dry or iron styling before an important day

Haircut FAQ

How much is a men's cut?
A men's cut is ₩18,000, a men's design cut ₩25,000 and a men's detox cut ₩40,000. A down cut is ₩40,000; adding the back is +₩20,000.
How much is a women's cut?
A women's cut is ₩20,000, a design cut (shampoo included) ₩30,000 and a detox cut ₩40,000. A bangs trim is ₩5,000.
Do you cut children's hair?
Yes. A junior cut (up to middle school) is ₩16,000 and a kids' cut (under 5) is ₩25,000.
