Finding the salon

We are 361 m from Exit 2 of Yangpyeong Station on Subway Line 5. Walk straight from Exit 2, and at the intersection we are across from Paris Baguette, on the 3rd floor of the building next to Yonsei ENT Clinic and Monomart. There is no parking at the salon; paid public parking is available at Yangpyeong Station Exit 2.
