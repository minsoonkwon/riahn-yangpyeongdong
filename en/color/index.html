From root color
to ombré and balayage

At RIAHN Hair Yangpyeong-dong, root color is ₩45,000 Basic / ₩60,000 Premium, and full color ₩87,500 Basic (₩70,000 via Naver Booking) / ₩112,500 Premium (₩90,000 via Naver Booking). Black color removal and toning are ₩62,500 each.

Root bleach is ₩65,000 Basic / ₩80,000 Premium, and full bleach ₩112,500 Basic / ₩137,500 Premium. Two-tone Basic is ₩112,500, sombré & weaving ₩137,500, and ombré & balayage ₩162,500. Your designer consults with you first, then styles to suit the look you want.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants to touch up grown-out roots
  • Anyone who wants to lift dark dye to a brighter color
  • Anyone who wants an accent with two-tone, ombré or balayage
  • Anyone who wants toning after bleach

FAQ

Hair Color FAQ

How much is full color?
Full color is ₩87,500 Basic and ₩112,500 Premium, or ₩70,000 and ₩90,000 when booked on Naver Booking.
How much is root color?
Root color is ₩45,000 Basic and ₩60,000 Premium. Root bleach is ₩65,000 Basic and ₩80,000 Premium.
Until what time can I get color?
Color services are available 10:00–18:30. The salon is open daily 10:00–20:00.
