About

Near Yangpyeong Station Exit 2,
RIAHN Hair Yangpyeong-dong

RIAHN Hair Yangpyeong-dong is a hair salon at 3F, 28 Yeongdeungpo-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul, close to Exit 2 of Yangpyeong Station. Walk straight from Exit 2, and at the intersection we are across from Paris Baguette, on the 3rd floor of the building next to Yonsei ENT Clinic and Monomart.

You can read about many guests' experiences in our 1,357 reviews. We specialize in popular styles such as dandy cuts, and also offer scalp treatments and head spa.

Opened in 2024 with 6 styling chairs, we are open daily 10:00–20:00 and closed on the day of Seollal and Chuseok. First-time guests receive a discount coupon for perms and color.

Why RIAHN Hair Yangpyeong-dong

  • 1,357 reviews to help you see what other guests think.
  • Designers who specialize in popular styles such as dandy cuts.
  • Scalp treatments and head spa alongside our hair services.
  • A perm and color discount coupon for first-time guests.
