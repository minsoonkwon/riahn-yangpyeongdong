FAQ

FAQ

Common questions about booking, parking, hours, service times, prices, discounts and booking a designer by name.

Home/FAQ
How do I book?
You can book your preferred date and time on Naver Booking. You can also book or ask by phone (+82-2-2677-3999).
Is parking available?
There is no parking at the salon; paid public parking is available at Yangpyeong Station Exit 2.
What are your hours and days off?
We are open daily 10:00–20:00 and closed on the day of Seollal and Chuseok.
Where is the salon?
3F, 28 Yeongdeungpo-ro, Yeongdeungpo-gu, Seoul. We are 361 m from Exit 2 of Yangpyeong Station on Subway Line 5. Walk straight from Exit 2, and at the intersection we are across from Paris Baguette, on the 3rd floor of the building next to Yonsei ENT Clinic and Monomart.
Until what time can I get color or a perm?
Color services are available 10:00–18:30, and perms (except heat perms and straightening) 10:00–18:00. If you have a preferred time, please call us (+82-2-2677-3999).
What are your prices?
Women's cut ₩20,000–30,000, men's cut ₩18,000–25,000, regular perm ₩100,000 (Basic), volume straightening ₩162,500 (Basic), root color ₩45,000 (Basic), full color ₩87,500 (Basic) and head spa ₩50,000. The larger amount in the price list is the regular price. Prices can vary with the service, hair volume and length, so we confirm the exact price at your consultation.
Is the price different if I book on Naver?
Yes. Some services, such as perms and straightening, full color and bleach, and Sweety care, are available at the smaller price shown when you book on Naver Booking. For example, a regular perm (Basic) is ₩100,000, or ₩80,000 via Naver Booking.
Is there a first-visit discount?
First-time guests receive a discount coupon for perms and color. Please check the coupon details on Naver Booking or call us (+82-2-2677-3999).
Is there a surcharge for long hair?
Yes. Short-cut length: none, bob length: +₩10,000, shoulder to chest: +₩20,000, below the chest: from +₩30,000. Please confirm which services it applies to at your consultation.
Can I book a specific designer?
Choose your preferred designer when you book. We recommend Senior Director Chihyeon for mid-length CS perms and shadow perms, Store Manager Jia for dandy and layered cuts, and Head Director Hyemin for dandy cuts and straightening.

Didn't find your answer? Call us during opening hours (daily 10:00–20:00).
