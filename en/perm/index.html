Perms & Straightening

Prices for regular and design perms, root, regular and volume straightening, setting perms, and down and partial perms.

Home/Perms & Straightening

From regular and setting perms
to volume straightening and down perms

At RIAHN Hair Yangpyeong-dong, a basic perm is ₩87,500 (₩70,000 via Naver Booking), a regular perm is ₩100,000 Basic / ₩112,500 Premium, and a design perm ₩137,500 Basic / ₩150,000 Premium. Setting perms start at ₩137,500 and straightening + setting at ₩175,000 (Basic).

Straightening starts at ₩150,000 and volume straightening at ₩162,500 (Basic), and root straightening (based on 4 cm) at ₩125,000. Straightening comes in three levels: Basic, Premium and Crystal. A down perm is ₩25,000 and a sides & back down perm ₩45,000, and we also offer partial perms such as bangs and root perms. Your designer consults with you first, then styles to suit the look you want. To change your color as well, see our hair color page.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants sleek hair with straightening or volume straightening
  • Anyone who wants natural waves with a setting perm
  • Anyone who wants to tame puffy sides and back with a down perm
  • Anyone who wants a partial perm on the bangs or roots only

FAQ

Perm FAQ

How much is volume straightening?
Volume straightening is ₩162,500 Basic, ₩187,500 Premium and ₩212,500 Crystal, or ₩130,000, ₩150,000 and ₩170,000 when booked on Naver Booking.
How much is a down perm?
A down perm is ₩25,000, and a sides & back down perm is ₩45,000.
Until what time can I get a perm?
Perms (except heat perms and straightening) are available 10:00–18:00. The salon is open daily 10:00–20:00.
