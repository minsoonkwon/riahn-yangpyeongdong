From scalp care and head spa
to hair care

RIAHN Hair Yangpyeong-dong offers scalp treatments and head spa. A scalp ampoule is ₩20,000, scalp scaling ₩35,000 and a head spa ₩50,000.

Hair care: soft care ₩20,000, basic partial care ₩60,000, basic full care ₩100,000, premium partial care ₩80,000 and premium full care ₩130,000. Sweety care is ₩250,000 (S), ₩300,000 (M) and ₩350,000 (L), or ₩175,000, ₩210,000 and ₩245,000 when booked on Naver Booking. To pair a treatment with a perm or color, see our perms & straightening page.

Recommended for

  • Anyone who wants a refreshed, clean scalp
  • Anyone who wants to relax with a head spa
  • Anyone who wants to smooth their hair after a perm or color
